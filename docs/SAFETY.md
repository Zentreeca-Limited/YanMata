# Safety, privacy and clinical governance

This is an engineering/product control plan, not a legal compliance opinion or clinical protocol. Obtain Nigeria-specific privacy, professional-practice and child-consent review before real-user enrolment. The audience is confirmed as ages 9 through the end of life. Age-appropriate operating and minor-access protocols remain to be defined. The demo health-services directory is synthetic; live listings require source ownership, verification dates and a correction process. External booking and automated emergency referral remain outside the current scope.

## Clinical information

Doctors/nurses available to the founder are a starting review network; record named accountable reviewers, verified registration, relevant scope of practice, conflict of interest and review capacity. Institution names are not partnership claims without permission/agreement.

Each health article and answer must identify original sources, author, reviewer, clinical revision, target audience, review date and next review deadline. Prefer current WHO, Nigerian public-health authorities and appropriate professional guidelines. Local clinicians approve applicability, help-seeking guidance and translations. General product documents do not substitute for clinical approval.

No unvalidated cancer-risk gauges or percentage predictions from the supplied wireframe. Do not infer hormonal imbalance, PCOS, pregnancy or diagnosis from a diary. Cycle estimates require documented inputs, minimum-history and uncertainty rules approved by a clinician; no fertility guarantees. Avoid normalising serious symptoms with generic reassurance. Clinicians define urgent-help pathways and maintain verified local contacts; the service must not promise emergency monitoring.

## Child safeguarding and shared devices

Within the confirmed age-9-plus audience, define age-appropriate experiences, age-assurance proportionality, consent/assent and guardian involvement based on local legal/clinical advice and actual user risk. Do not default to a single guessed legal consent age. Separate caregiver education from a child's private health account. No automatic guardian access to journals or chats; establish lawful, safe access rules and explain confidentiality limits.

Allow anonymous educational browsing and optional profile fields. Underage accounts, professional messaging and collection of sensitive child records remain gated until the protocol and trained safeguarding owner exist. No public child profiles, unsolicited messages, adult peer matching or precise location sharing. Professional access is verified, assigned, time-limited and reportable. Define abuse reporting, escalation, response windows, retention and specialist supervision before launch.

Default notification copy is generic. App lock and app-switcher privacy cover protect casual exposure; disclose limits on compromised/shared devices. No false promise that a quick-exit button erases OS/browser history. Users can turn off reminders and use manual location search without losing education access.

## Data controls

Maintain a data inventory mapping every field to purpose, access role, legal basis, retention, storage region, processor and deletion method. Separate core service processing from optional research/analytics/AI consent. Allow withdrawal without coercive prompts; explain what historical information must legally be retained if applicable. Never use private journals to train models by default.

Encrypt transport, databases, backups and local clinical storage; use managed keys with rotation and least-privilege access. Separate identity from health tables where useful, but do not claim that pseudonyms anonymise data. Keep media private unless explicitly approved for public publication. Export links are authenticated, short-lived and audited. Deletion covers derived stores, search indexes, media and restored backups under a documented retention procedure.

Threat model: stolen/shared device; coercive household access; hostile account; dishonest staff/provider; broken object-level authorisation; compromised dependency/secret; leaked notification; malicious upload; stale cached guidance; unintended AI data disclosure; sync replay. For each, record preventative control, detection, owner and residual risk before production.

No session replay on health routes, advertising trackers, sensitive URL query strings or health text in analytics/logs/crash reports. Redact request bodies and allowlist operational telemetry. Security audit logs retain actor/action/resource IDs and time, not unnecessary journal/message content. Review any third-party AI/media/analytics processing and cross-border transfers before enablement.

## Bounded intelligence

Proposed pilot research feature: multilingual semantic retrieval from approved content, with source citations and deterministic safety routing. Start with ranked approved passages; generated explanations are optional and feature-gated. Treat retrieved content/user messages as untrusted input, prevent tool execution from text, validate citations against actual passage IDs and abstain when evidence is insufficient. No autonomous prescriptions or diagnosis.

Evaluation set must cover local-language wording, adolescent questions, emergencies, misinformation, missing evidence and adversarial prompts. Clinicians score factuality, citation support, harmful omissions, age appropriateness and escalation. Record corpus/model versions, latency/cost and failure rates; thresholds require clinical sign-off before public enablement. Model choice follows measured quality, privacy, deployment and cost, not a “best model” marketing claim. Offline users receive reviewed cached material, not invented offline AI answers.

## Operational gates

Name clinical, safeguarding, security and privacy owners. Verify specialists and recheck expiry; provide report/block and complaint review. Document breach response, content withdrawal, provider suspension, urgent-help contact verification and service shutdown procedure. Conduct restore and incident exercises. A completed checklist without evidence is not production readiness.
